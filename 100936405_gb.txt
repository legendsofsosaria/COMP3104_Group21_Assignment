George Brown College - Casa Loma Campus

I'm in my final year at George Brown College, and I'm mostly on the Casa Loma campus or at home.
It's up on Kendal Ave in Toronto, close to the actual Casa Loma castle,
and it's where a lot of the tech programs are. 
It's a pretty small campus, which makes it easy to find your classes and your people.

I picked a tech program because I like building things and figuring out how they work.
 George Brown's hands-on style fits how I learn. 
 Working in teams on projects like KitchenSync and Kinook has been one of the best parts of my final year.
