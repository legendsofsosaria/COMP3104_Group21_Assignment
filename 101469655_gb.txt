George Brown Polytechnic

A public college in Toronto, Ontario offering a variety of programs that are career oriented.

The school has 3 downtown Toronto campuses
- Casa Loma Campus
- St. James Campus
- Waterfront Campus

The primary faculties/schools are:
- Faculty of Applied Science, Construction, and Engineering Technology
    - Angelo DelZotto School of Construction Management
    - School of Apprenticeship and Skilled Trades
    - School of Architectural Studies
    - School of Computer Technology
    - School of Engineering Technologies

- Faculty of Business, Creative Industries, and Culinary Arts
    - School of Design
    - School of Management
    - School of Marketing and Financial Services
    - School of Media and Performing Arts
    - The Chef School

- Faculty of Community Services and Interdisciplinary Studies
    - School of Behavioural Science
    - School of Deaf and Deafblind Studies
    - School of Early Childhood
    - School of ESL and Immigrant Education
    - School of Liberal Arts and Sciences
    - School of Social and Community Services
    - School of Work and College Preparation

- Faculty of Health Sciences
    - Sally Horsfall Eaton School of Nursing
    - School of Allied Health
    - School of Dental Health
