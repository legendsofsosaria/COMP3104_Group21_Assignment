The importance of DevOps in the software development lifecycle

DevOps is like the glue that holds the development and operations teams together.

When applied correctly DevOps can:
- Reduce IT overhead
- Remove mundane manual tasks
- Enforce best security
- Expedite delivery of products
- Improve the communication and collaboration across teams

DevOps Best Practices:
1) Agile Project Management
2) Shift left with CI/CD
3) Build with the right tools
4) Implement automation
5) Monitor the DevOps pipeline and applications
6) Observability
7) Gather continuous feedback
8) Change the culture
